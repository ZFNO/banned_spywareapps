# THE MANIFESTO

> they asked for a script. we gave them a **lifestyle**.

tiktok does not own my thumbs.
instagram does not own my retinas.
facebook does not own my 3am.
applovin does not own my battery.

we are not "limiting screen time."
we are performing **surgery**.

no root. no xposed. no magisk. no copium.
just `adb`, a `chmod`, and an unshakeable hatred of background services.

they wanted infinite scroll.
we said: `am force-stop`.
they wanted autostart on boot.
we said: `pm disable-user`.
they wanted push notifications.
we said: `appops set ... RUN_ANY_IN_BACKGROUND deny`.

this repo runs on exactly one phone and it will run on yours too.
the folder is `0700`. the logs are `0600`. the vibes are immaculate.
`run_all.sh` re-arms the locks every single time because git refuses to
remember what a file mode is, coward that it is.

**the apps will not phone home.
they will not wake the phone.
they will not exist unless you summon them,
and the moment you leave,
they are gone.**

add an app. ban the app. log the ban. move on.

— signed, the ninth circle of `adb shell`

*P.S. Messenger is opt-in because we are unhinged, not unreasonable.*
